How it runs

  1. Sign up with your Riot ID

    Click Register on the announcement and enter your Riot ID. That's the whole signup.

  2. Get verified

    Starling confirms the account through the Riot API and pulls your rank and match history. Brand-new accounts get a second look before they go into the draft.

  3. Get drafted

    Teams are balanced by estimated MMR so games stay close, and you're placed in the role you asked for whenever the numbers allow it.

  4. Play the bracket

    Matches run back to back through the evening. Moderators report results in Discord and the bracket updates as you go.

Everyone gets a second life

Every tournament is double elimination. Losing a match drops you into the losers bracket, where you can fight all the way back to the Grand Finals. Starling draws the bracket and posts an updated image after every game.

Before you join

Common questions

Do I need a full team to sign up?

No. Most entrants register solo. Starling verifies your Riot account and drafts you onto a balanced team of five, so showing up without a premade squad, or without knowing anyone at all, is normal, not a workaround.

Is it actually free?

Yes. Every tournament is free to enter. No downloads, no entry fee, no catch.

What do you actually win?

It varies by event, and it is always something small and in-game, covered directly by the server owner. There is no entry fee either way, and prizes are expected to grow as the community does.

What rank do I need to be?

Any. Teams are matched by estimated MMR, not a rank cutoff, so Iron and Challenger players both get competitive, evenly matched games.

How are teams balanced?

Starling estimates each player's MMR from their ranked history and drafts teams so both sides come in close in total strength, then seats people in the role they asked for whenever the numbers allow it.

What if I lose my first match?

You're not out. Every tournament is double elimination, so a loss drops you into the losers bracket, not out of the night. You can fight all the way back to the Grand Finals.

I missed registration. What now?

Registration closes once teams are drafted, usually a few days before the event. Join the server anyway: you'll be in the room when the next one opens, and events run biweekly on Saturdays.

I got banned. Can I appeal?

Yes. Appeals go through a short form, not a mod DM, because Discord won't let a banned member message someone they no longer share a server with. The form link isn't live on this page yet; check the server's rules or announcements channel for the current process.
