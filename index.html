    How it runs

    1. Sign up with your Riot ID

      Every event goes up as an announcement in the server. Click Register on it and enter your Riot ID. That's the whole signup.

    2. Get verified

      Starling confirms the account through the Riot API and pulls your rank and match history. Brand-new accounts get a second look before they go into the draft.

    3. Get drafted

      Teams are balanced by estimated MMR, the matchmaking rating read off your ranked history, so games stay close. You're placed in the role you asked for whenever the numbers allow it.

    4. Play the bracket

      Matches run back to back through the evening. Moderators report results in Discord and the bracket updates as you go.

    5. Honor your teammates

      When the night ends, everyone gets a quick vote on the people they played with. That reputation follows a player into the next event, so how you treat four strangers actually counts for something here.

    Everyone gets a second life

      Common questions

      Do I need a full team to sign up?

      No. Register solo or with one duo partner, and Starling drafts you onto a balanced team of five. Showing up without a squad is normal, not a workaround. Clan tournaments for full premade teams are coming.

      What if I get bad teammates?

      Every event ends with an honor vote on the people you played with, and that reputation follows a player into the next one, so behaviour here has a memory. Accounts that look stronger than they claim get a smurf check before the draft, and organisers can hold someone out of an event over their record.

      Is it actually free?

      Yes. There will always be free tournaments on the schedule. Paid events with bigger prizes may join the lineup down the line, but free entry isn't going away.

      What do you actually win?

      Some events are just for the games, no prize attached. Others carry one, and when they do, it's named right on the registration announcement so you know what's on the line before you sign up.

      What rank do I need to be?

      Any. Teams are matched by estimated MMR, not rank, so Iron and Challenger both get competitive games.

      How are teams balanced?

      Starling estimates your MMR from ranked history and drafts teams to come in close in strength, seating you in your preferred role when possible.

      What if I lose my first match?

      You're not out. It's double elimination: a loss sends you to the losers bracket, with a path back to the Grand Finals.

      I missed registration. What now?

      Registration opens shortly after the previous event and stays open for about a week, well before the next one. You can still register as a substitute at any time, even after sign-ups close, and you'll get a DM if a spot opens up. Otherwise, join the server anyway: events run every three weeks on Saturdays, so you'll be ready when it opens again.

      I got banned. Can I appeal?

      Yes. Email appeals@starling.gg with your Discord username and we'll take a look.
